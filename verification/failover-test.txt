HSRP Failover Test Results
==========================================================

Test Date: 2026-09-30
Tester: Armin Isa
Project: hsrp-redundancy-lab

----------------------------------------------------------
Test Scenario:
----------------------------------------------------------
Verify that the network remains operational when the 
primary gateway (R1) fails.

----------------------------------------------------------
Test Procedure:
----------------------------------------------------------
1. Before failure, verify HSRP status on both routers:
   - R1 is Active for VLAN 10 (Priority 150)
   - R2 is Standby for VLAN 10 (Priority 100)

2. Start a continuous ping from PC1 to PC2:
   PC1> ping 192.168.20.10

3. Simulate R1 failure by shutting down its LAN interface:
   R1(config)# interface GigabitEthernet1/0
   R1(config-if)# shutdown

4. Observe HSRP state change and ping continuity.

5. Restore R1:
   R1(config-if)# no shutdown

----------------------------------------------------------
Expected Result:
----------------------------------------------------------
- R2 should transition from Standby to Active for VLAN 10
- Ping should continue with minimal disruption
- After R1 recovery, R1 should reclaim Active role (preempt)

----------------------------------------------------------
Actual Result:
----------------------------------------------------------
- R2 successfully became Active for VLAN 10.
- Continuous ping showed at most 1-2 packet drops during 
  the failover transition (approximately 10 seconds).
- After R1 was restored, it reclaimed Active role due to 
  the "standby 10 preempt" command.

----------------------------------------------------------
Conclusion:
----------------------------------------------------------
HSRP failover is working as designed. The network achieved 
high availability with minimal downtime (< 10 seconds), 
which is acceptable for enterprise environments.
